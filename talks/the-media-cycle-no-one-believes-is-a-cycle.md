I've built through three waves of media: streaming at Hulu, the fight for the living room, and creators becoming the platform at Packagd and Meta. Each one arrived with the same conviction that this time is different.

The companies that keep getting blindsided usually lived through the last cycle and never noticed its shape. This talk lays that shape out, and shows where today's AI wave sits on it.
