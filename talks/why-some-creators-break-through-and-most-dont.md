Most platforms and brands still pick creators on follower count and volume. Neither predicts who'll still be building in five years.

I've seen this from both sides. At Packagd we built for creators and got it wrong before we got it right. At SuperBam I worked the IP-protection side and saw which creators had built something worth protecting. In between came a hundred-plus Building Value conversations, from a corporate chef who found TikTok to a YouTuber's long climb to 100K subscribers. The same few patterns separate who breaks through, and none of them are about production value.
