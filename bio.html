01 · The longer story

Why behind
the career.

The part a résumé leaves out.

When I was 19, doctors found Stage 2B Hodgkin's lymphoma. I was at Northwestern, a theatre major, a lot more at home onstage than in an exam room. I did the chemo and the radiation and finished school anyway — then moved to Los Angeles instead of back to the East Coast, because by then playing it safe had a cost I could actually feel.

"Someday" stopped being a safe assumption that year. It never really came back.

That urgency turned into a pattern: find something worth building and get to it before the rest of the room realizes it's worth building. It took me through Hulu's early years, a company I co-founded that Facebook acquired, a stint building Live Shopping inside Meta as the team's first Founder in Residence, and eventually the Interim CEO seat at SuperBam.

I went to Northwestern to be an actor. That is not a detail I bring up to be charming about it — it's half of how I work. Four years of theatre and improv is four years of training in reading a room: who has stopped talking, what the scene is actually about underneath the words everyone agreed to say, when to hold a beat instead of filling it. I spent the next twenty years watching enormous things scale and paying attention to which decisions made people money and which ones quietly cost them. The useful part is the two together. Plenty of operators can read a P&L. Fewer can tell you what the room isn't saying while you read it.

None of it followed a strategy document. It followed a read on systems — which ones were about to break open, which ones needed someone to care more carefully than the people already in charge. I think in stories before slides, and I lead with the narrative. That frustrates the people who want a deck before they'll engage with the problem. It works on everyone else.

In April 2025 I moved the family to the south of France. Not a sabbatical, not a gesture — a bet that the life we'd been optimizing for in LA wasn't the one we actually wanted. I write, advise, and build from here now. Two kids who think a Tuesday market is normal are the best evidence it's working.

Co-founded Packagd. Facebook acquired it in 2019, where the team built Live Shopping for the Commerce group and a native gift-certificate platform that gave small businesses a way to take cash during lockdown. After moving to Meta's New Product Experimentation team, I led Super, a live streaming app, and became the team's first Founder in Residence — a startup operator's seat inside a very large machine.
