01 · The longer story

What the résumé
leaves out.

When I was 19, doctors found Stage 2B Hodgkin's lymphoma. I was at Northwestern, a theatre major, a lot more at home onstage than in an exam room. I did the chemo and the radiation and finished school anyway. Then I moved to Los Angeles instead of back to the East Coast, because by then playing it safe had a cost I could actually feel.

That urgency turned into a habit: find something worth building and get there before the rest of the room notices. It runs through everything in the log below, from Hulu's early years to the interim CEO seat at SuperBam.

I went to Northwestern to be an actor. I don't mention it to be charming. It's half of how I work. Four years of theatre and improv is four years of training in reading a room: who has stopped talking, what the scene is actually about underneath the words everyone agreed to say, when to hold a beat instead of filling it. I spent the next twenty years inside things as they scaled, paying attention to which decisions made people money and which ones quietly cost them. The useful part is having both.

Plenty of operators can read a P&L. Fewer can tell you what the room isn't saying while you read it.

The through-line has been a read on systems: which ones were about to break open, and which ones needed someone to care more carefully than the people already in charge. I think in stories before slides. That frustrates the people who want a deck before they'll engage with the problem. It works on everyone else.

In April 2025 I moved the family to the south of France. We bet that the life we'd been optimizing for in LA wasn't the one we actually wanted. I write, advise, and build from here now. Two kids who think a Tuesday market is normal are the best evidence it's working.

Co-founded Packagd. The decision that mattered was killing a finished iPhone app nobody was using and rebuilding around the creators themselves. That produced the relationships, and sixty of the top hundred tech creators on YouTube, that made the rest possible. After Facebook acquired the company in 2019, the team built Live Shopping for the Commerce group and a native gift-certificate platform that gave small businesses a way to take cash during lockdown. Then, on Meta's New Product Experimentation team, I built and launched Super, a live streaming app, and became the team's first Founder in Residence: a startup operator's seat inside a very large machine.
