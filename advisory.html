04 How I work

The terms, before you ask.

  • I'm not here to fix your culture.

    I'm here to name what's standing between your team and the money, and stay until it moves. If what's in the way turns out to be how you work, we'll deal with that — but the target is always the number.

  • Narrative first.

    I think in stories before slides and I lead with the argument. We start from a clear written case so the team can weigh the assumptions, trade-offs, and the decision together.

  • Fixed fee, scoped at the start.

    No hourly billing and no surprise invoices. We agree the question and the deliverable before any money moves.

  • I do the work.

    No associates, no junior team, no one learning on your budget. You get me, and you get me in the room.

  • Confidential by default.

    No NDA for the first conversation. For project work, a standard mutual NDA within 24 hours.

  • Remote-first, Cannes-based.

    Mornings overlap with the US East Coast. I travel in Europe when the work needs a room, and it's a train, not a flight.

  • I'll say no fast.

    If I'm not the right person, you'll hear it in the first conversation, with a name of someone who is.
