02 Who it's for

Three moments I'm useful in.

Timing matters more than company size. If one of these is where you are this quarter, we should talk.

001

You're raising

The story has to survive investors who've heard a hundred creator-economy pitches this year. Your own team has to agree on it first, and right now they'd each tell a different version.

002

You're deciding what the company is

The roadmap turned into a wish list (the creator tool, the licensing deal, the FAST channel) and every item on it is somebody's favorite. Someone has to say which things die, and it can't be the person who has to work with everyone on Monday.

003

You're mid-transition

A leader left, two teams merged, or a platform changed its terms overnight. The work now is holding it steady and keeping people moving while the new shape gets decided.

+ Before any money moves

The first two weeks are the same for everyone.

Every engagement starts with a call, and the engagement itself is the last thing we get to.

  1. The first call

    Ninety minutes, free. You walk me through everything that's in the way, and I take notes.

  2. I read

    Your calendar, your tools, what you're telling investors, whatever reports you actually run the company from. Send all of it. We sign a mutual NDA before I open any of it.

  3. The assessment

    A written read of what I see: what's in the way, what I'd do first, what nobody's saying, and the cadence we'd work to.

  4. The agreement

    If the assessment is right, you sign it and we start. If it isn't, you keep it, and I'll tell you who to call instead.

04 How I work

The terms, before you ask.

  • I'm not here to fix your culture.

    I'm here to find what's standing between your team and the money, and stay until it moves. If that turns out to be how you work, we'll deal with it, and the number stays the target.

  • Fixed fee, scoped at the start.

    The prices on this page are the prices. You won't get an hourly bill or a surprise invoice, and the assessment sets scope and cadence before any money moves.

  • Reachable, with a stated turnaround.

    Text me and you'll hear back inside 24 hours, unless I've told you ahead of time that I'm away. I'm in Cannes, so my mornings overlap the US East Coast. Emergency calls can happen at any hour with a little warning.

  • I do the work.

    There's no associate or junior team behind me learning on your budget. You get me, in the room.

  • Confidential by default.

    The first conversation needs no paperwork. Before I read a single document of yours, we sign a standard mutual NDA, mine or yours, within 24 hours.
