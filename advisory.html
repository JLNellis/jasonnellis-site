+ Before any money moves

The first two weeks are the same for everyone.

You don't buy an engagement on this page. You ask for a call, and the engagement is the last thing we get to.

  1. The first call

    Ninety minutes, free. You talk about everything that's in the way. I listen and take notes. Nothing gets pitched.

  2. I read

    Your calendar, your tools, what you're telling investors, whatever reports you actually run the company from. Send it all — under a mutual NDA, signed before I open any of it.

  3. The assessment

    A written read of what I see: what's in the way, what I'd do first, what nobody's saying, and the cadence we'd work to.

  4. The agreement

    If the assessment is right, you sign it and we start. If it isn't, you keep it, and I'll tell you who to call instead.

04 How I work

The terms, before you ask.

  • I'm not here to fix your culture.

    I'm here to name what's standing between your team and the money, and stay until it moves. If what's in the way turns out to be how you work, we'll deal with that — but the target is always the number.

  • Fixed fee, scoped at the start.

    No hourly billing and no surprise invoices. The prices on this page are the prices; the assessment sets the scope and the cadence before any money moves.

  • Reachable, with a stated turnaround.

    Text me. You'll hear back inside 24 hours except where I've told you otherwise, and I'll tell you in advance when I'm unavailable. I'm in Cannes, so mornings overlap the US East Coast, and emergency calls happen with a little warning whatever the time zone.

  • I do the work.

    No associates, no junior team, no one learning on your budget. You get me, and you get me in the room.

  • Confidential by default.

    No NDA for the first conversation. Before I read a single document of yours, a standard mutual NDA — mine or yours, signed within 24 hours.
